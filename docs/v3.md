### Updated Category / Type Taxonomy

Six new categories have been added to the original taxonomy to capture previously missing institutional functions:

-   **Law Enforcement – Specialist Unit:** Operational police/gendarmerie unit with dedicated hate crime or diversity investigative mandate.
-   **Law Enforcement – National Coordination/Bureau:** Higher-tier law enforcement body providing cross-jurisdictional support, methodology, or complex case investigation rather than frontline response.
-   **Law Enforcement – Specialist Role:** Designated officer(s) within general units performing hate crime/diversity functions without constituting a standalone unit (common in smaller jurisdictions).
-   **Police Oversight Body:** Independent statutory body specifically mandated to investigate complaints against police and/or review policing practices.
-   **Independent Oversight / NHRI:** National Human Rights Institution or equivalent independent body with broad human rights mandate, typically accredited under Paris Principles.
-   **Statutory/Government Equality Body:** State-established body with specific mandate to combat discrimination, receive complaints, and promote equality (distinct from general human rights bodies).
-   **Third-Party Reporting Centre / NGO:** Civil society organization formally or informally designated to receive hate crime/incident reports as an alternative to direct police contact.
-   **Civil Society / Advocacy & Support:** Non-governmental organization providing victim support, legal aid, policy advocacy, community services, or monitoring.
-   **Internal Staff Network / Liaison:** Organization or network composed of serving law enforcement/court personnel advocating for LGBTQI+ staff and improving institutional culture.
-   **Government Coordination Body:** Executive branch entity coordinating cross-ministerial or cross-agency policy without direct investigative or adjudicatory powers.
-   **International/Pan-European Body:** Supranational organization providing monitoring, data aggregation, technical assistance, or advocacy across multiple jurisdictions.
-   **Prosecutorial Specialist Unit** *(NEW):* Dedicated prosecutor(s) or coordination unit within prosecution service ensuring consistent charging, sentencing, and evidentiary standards for bias-motivated offences. Critical bridge between police investigation and judicial outcome.
-   **Judicial Training / Council Body** *(NEW):* Institution responsible for mandatory or voluntary training of judges/magistrates on hate crime sentencing, LGBTQI+ competency, and bias awareness. Directly affects courtroom experience and case outcomes.
-   **Data / Statistical & Research Agency** *(NEW):* Official statistical office or independent research institute producing hate crime data, victimization surveys, and evaluative studies. Enables evidence-based policy and accountability measurement.
-   **Health / Social Service** *(NEW):* Public health or social welfare body providing LGBTQI+-competent care, trauma-informed victim support, and referral pathways intersecting with justice system. Addresses consequences of hate crime beyond criminal prosecution.
-   **Academic / Research Institute** *(NEW):* University-affiliated or independent research body conducting empirical studies on hate crime prevalence, policing effectiveness, and LGBTQI+ experiences. Feeds evidence into policy and practice distinct from official statistics.
-   **Trade Union / Professional Association Body** *(NEW):* Labour or professional organization addressing workplace discrimination, hate crime affecting members, and LGBTQI+ employment rights. Covers gaps left by employer-based and state-based mechanisms.

### Key Additions Explained

-   **Baltic States (Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania):** Previously entirely absent despite having active hate crime challenges, EU membership obligations, and significant Russian-speaking minority dynamics affecting LGBTQI+ safety.
-   **Central/Eastern Europe (Poland, Czech Republic, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Slovenia):** Added prosecutorial units, restored equality bodies (Poland), and parallel civil society oversight (Hungary) that were missing from prior versions.
-   **Western Balkans (Montenegro, North Macedonia, Albania, BiH, Kosovo):** All EU candidate/potential candidate states where conditionality-driven institutional creation is ongoing but previously unmapped.
-   **Malta, Cyprus, Luxembourg:** Small EU states with distinct institutional architectures previously omitted despite having specialized bodies.
-   **Prosecutorial Units:** Added across nearly all jurisdictions because charging/prosecution decisions are the single largest attrition point in hate crime justice pathways. Without mapping these, the directory presented only half the criminal justice chain.
-   **Judicial Training Bodies:** Added because courtroom experience and sentencing outcomes depend entirely on judicial competence, which is institutionally separate from both police and prosecution.
-   **Statistical Agencies:** Added because effectiveness measurement is impossible without knowing who produces the data. Official statistics offices are distinct from both police recording and NGO shadow reporting.
-   **Eurojust & EJTN:** Added as pan-European judicial cooperation and training bodies that directly affect cross-border hate crime prosecution and judicial competence—previously only executive/police-side international bodies were listed.
